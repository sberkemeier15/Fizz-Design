Event Poster / Print Design

Highball Cincy Birthday Bash Poster

Promotional poster design created for a live birthday concert event at Highball Cincy supporting a charitable cause. The design focused on visual hierarchy, energetic typography, and capturing the atmosphere of Cincinnati’s local music scene.

Summershake Down Poster

Designed and illustrated a promotional poster for a live music fundraiser at Southgate House Revival. The project involved creating custom band-inspired character illustrations, organizing event information through strong visual hierarchy, and developing a cohesive design that balances entertainment branding with charity-focused messaging.
